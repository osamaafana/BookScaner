# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Readers standing in front of a shelf: a bookstore, a book sale, a library, a friend's house. Phone in one hand, often
in mixed indoor light, a few minutes at most. They see dozens of spines they don't recognize and want a fast answer to
"which of these would I actually enjoy?". Desktop visitors (people trying the demo) are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose
BookScanner reads every spine in one photo, turns each into a real book (title, author, cover, details), and ranks them
against the reader's taste. Readers keep a reading list and a scan history. Success: photo to a short, trustworthy list
of picks in under a minute, with no typing.

## Brand Personality
Calm, smart, bookish. Quiet confidence: the books are the hero and the AI stays in the background. Voice is plain and
warm, like a well-read friend, never hype ("Transform your…", "Advanced AI"). Feel reference: Apple Books (cover-led,
clean, calm, native-feeling).

## Anti-references
- The generic AI-startup look: purple-to-blue gradients, glowing dots, blurred orbs, gradient text, giant hype headlines.
- Plain admin dashboards: gray boxes, data tables, stat tiles.
- Fake effects: pretend "detected" boxes or progress that does not reflect what the system is really doing.

## Design Principles
1. Books first: covers and titles carry the screen; chrome and decoration step back.
2. One-handed and fast: the main action is always reachable with a thumb, every screen has one obvious next step.
3. Honest feedback: show real progress and real results; never simulate.
4. Quiet intelligence: explain the AI's picks briefly in plain words instead of advertising the AI.
5. Calm by default: motion only when it explains a change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus, full keyboard use, labelled controls for screen readers, 44px touch
targets. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (crossfade or instant). Never use color alone to show status.
